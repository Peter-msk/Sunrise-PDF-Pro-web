## Scope
Opt-in buildDirectPdf only, preserving DEV52 geometry-only API. Consume legacy textRun placement, positions and UTF8/UTF16 clusters, preserve text/rectangle flow order, convert px to pt once. Narrow regular static TrueType, horizontal precomposed Hangul and selected ASCII only, unchanged verbatim source. Explicit caller-approved local font bytes, parsed family/PostScript/subfamily, SFNT OS/2 embedding bits, whole-run and isolated-glyph equality including offsets and advances. No new dependency or network/font fallback. Block unsupported text styles/projections, shaping, glyph variants, images, clipping, unsupported page operations, missing font/glyph and malformed positions. No partial success bytes. Bounded run/page/document text counts and font bytes. Not an implementation of general HWP shaping or original-font identity from upstream glyph evidence.
